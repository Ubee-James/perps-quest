The problem
01
They find out what liquidation means by hitting it
At 20× leverage, a 5% move against you wipes half your collateral. Most traders discover this the expensive way. The information existed — it just wasn't explained anywhere useful.
02
Funding rates drain accounts while people sleep
Every 8 hours, a fee is quietly paid between longs and shorts. Most traders don't know it exists until they check their balance a week later and wonder where it went.
03
The information exists — it's just buried
Scattered across YouTube videos, Reddit threads, and exchange documentation written for people who already know what they're reading. There's no clear path from curious to competent.
04
Reading isn't enough. You have to feel it.
You can read about leverage all day. The concept only really lands when you watch your liquidation price sitting on the chart as a red line, moving closer with every tick.

"The goal was never to make people better traders. It was to make sure they understood what they were getting into before they found out the hard way."

— Founder, Perps Quest
